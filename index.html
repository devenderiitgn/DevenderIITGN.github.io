EXPERIENCE

A timeline of work I'm proud of.

Jan 2026 — Present
Program Associate I
Alumni Relations Office, IIT Gandhinagar
Fundraising strategy & resource mobilization
  • Redesigned the institute's fundraising communication architecture — segmenting 4,500+ alumni by graduation year, giving history, and scholarship interest — lifting donor participation from ~3% to over 10% and driving contributions over ₹50 lakh.
  • Structured and secured seven new scholarships — two Batch Scholarships and five Alumni Endowed Scholarships — a repeatable framework for donor-funded student support.
  • Conceived and shipped a dedicated Alumni Endowed Scholarship website, giving donors a permanent, transparent record of philanthropic impact.
Financial tracking & systems
  • Authored a comprehensive CRM proposal to unify donor management, communications, event tracking, volunteer coordination, and fundraising workflows.
  • Owned end-to-end financial tracking of donor contributions, maintaining fund-allocation records and balance sheets for accountability.
  • Directed maintenance of a 5,400-record alumni database and built an automation pipeline refreshing data every 60–90 days.
New initiatives, brand & stakeholder engagement
  • Conceptualised and launched SETU, a student-to-alumni transition program — 600+ attendees, ~45% pledging. The Class of 2026 contributed over ₹11 lakh across initiatives.

  • Launched location- and batch-wise alumni networking communities on the institute's portal and app.
  • Ran the Alumni Relations stall at Convocation; recruited and managed 10–15 student volunteers across data, event, and engagement workstreams.
